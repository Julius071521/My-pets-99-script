Quick Customer Guide

Everything you need to get started in one place.

  1. Register — Click "Get Started" and create your free account. You'll receive a verification email.
  2. Top Up — Go to Dashboard → Wallet → Deposit. Choose GCash, Maya, or BPI and follow the instructions.
  3. Find a Service — Browse the service catalog. Use the search or platform filters to find what you need.
  4. Enter Details — Paste your public profile/post URL, enter the quantity, and review the price.
  5. Place Order — Click "Place Order". Your order is dispatched instantly and you can track it in Order History.
💡 Tip: Make sure your profile or post is set to Public before ordering, otherwise the service cannot be delivered.
  1. Open Dashboard — Click "Open Order Desk" from the homepage or log in to your account.
  2. Browse Catalog — Scroll through the full service list or use the search bar to find a specific service.
  3. Filter by Platform — Use the platform buttons (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, etc.) to narrow down options.
  4. Check Details — Click any service card to see delivery speed, quality notes, min/max quantity, and price per unit.
  5. Compare Prices — Services are sorted by quality. "Premium" services cost more but deliver better retention.
💡 Tip: Check the Global Order Feed above to see which services are completing fast vs. which are slow right now.
  1. Go to Order History — After logging in, click "Order History" in the dashboard sidebar.
  2. Find Your Order — Orders are listed from newest to oldest. Each shows Service Name, Quantity, Status, and Date.
  3. Check Status — Statuses explained:
    • 🟡 Pending — Order received, waiting to start
    • 🟢 Processing / In Progress — Being delivered right now
    • ✅ Completed — Fully delivered
    • ⚠️ Partial — Delivered what was possible, rest refunded
    • ❌ Canceled — Not started, full refund issued
  4. Processing Time — Most services complete in 0–72 hours depending on the service type and quantity.
  5. Stuck Order? — If your order is Pending/Processing for more than 48 hours, open a support ticket and we'll investigate.

📱 GCash

  1. Open GCash app → Send Money
  2. Enter our GCash number (shown inside dashboard)
  3. Enter the amount and complete the transfer
  4. Screenshot the confirmation and send to our WhatsApp or support ticket
  5. Wallet credited within 5–15 minutes

🏦 Maya / BPI

  1. Open Maya app or BPI Online → Transfer
  2. Use our account number shown in dashboard
  3. Transfer the amount and save the reference number
  4. Send reference number + amount to support
  5. BPI transfers may take 15–60 minutes during banking hours
💡 Minimum deposit: ₱50. No maximum. Wallet balance never expires.
  1. Support Ticket — Log in → Dashboard → Support Tickets → Create New Ticket. Best for order issues, refund requests, and deposit confirmations.
  2. WhatsApp — Click the WhatsApp button on this page for quick questions. Response time: usually under 1 hour during business hours.
  3. Order Issues — If your order is not delivered after 48 hours, open a ticket with your Order ID and we'll resolve it.
  4. Refunds — Eligible failed orders are refunded to your ApexBoost wallet automatically or via support request.
  5. Business Hours — We're most active 8AM–10PM PHT daily. Tickets are checked even on weekends.
💡 Tip: Always include your Order ID or transaction reference when messaging support — this speeds up resolution significantly.

📄 Terms & Conditions & Privacy Policy

1. General Agreement

By placing an order with ApexBoost, you automatically accept all the terms of service listed below, whether you read them or not. We reserve the right to change these terms without prior notice.

2. Boosting Services

ApexBoost services are strictly intended for promotional purposes to boost the online appearance of your social profiles. We do not guarantee active interactions or engagement from referred accounts. All delivery counts are estimations, and refilling is only guaranteed if specified in the service package guidelines.

3. Refund Policy

No refunds will be issued to your raw payment method. After a manual deposit has been verified and allocated to your dashboard balance, it cannot be reversed. If you create fraud disputes, we reserve the right to ban your account and suspend all active campaigns.

4. Account Guidelines

Do not submit duplicate or conflicting campaigns for the same target link until the first order is completely finished. Doing so will result in overlapping delivery, incorrect counts, and potential loss of credits without refunds.

5. Privacy Policy

Your privacy is extremely important to us. Any personal details provided during account registration, email verification, or balance top-ups (e.g. GCash references) are handled securely. We will never sell, lease, or distribute your private details to third parties, and all data is encrypted and transferred via SSL/TLS secure protocols.
