Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026  ·  ApexSMM Boosting

ApexBoost is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

Data TypeWhat We CollectWhy
Account DataUsername, email address, password (hashed)Account creation and authentication
Payment DataGCash/Maya/BPI reference numbers, deposit amountsDeposit verification only
Order DataSocial media URLs submitted for orders, service type, quantityService fulfillment
Device/IP DataIP address, browser type, device typeSecurity, fraud prevention
Communication DataSupport ticket messages, email correspondenceCustomer support

We do not collect your social media passwords, credit card numbers, or government IDs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your ApexBoost account
  • To process and fulfill your service orders
  • To verify deposits and manage your wallet balance
  • To send transactional emails (OTP, order updates, deposit confirmations)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • To improve our platform based on usage patterns

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • SMM Provider Partners (e.g., RKD Panel) — only your service URL and order details are transmitted, never your account information
  • Email Service Provider — to send transactional emails
  • Cloudflare — for DDoS protection, CDN, and bot filtering
  • Law Enforcement — if legally required by Philippine law

4. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. Order history is retained for 24 months for dispute resolution. IP logs are retained for 90 days for security purposes. You may request deletion of your account at any time (see Section 6).

5. Cookies & Tracking

ApexBoost uses:

  • Session tokens — stored in your browser's localStorage to keep you logged in
  • Cloudflare cookies — for bot detection and performance
  • Facebook Pixel — for marketing analytics (you can opt out via your Facebook settings)

We do not use third-party advertising cookies beyond what is listed above.

6. Your Rights

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — update incorrect information via your account settings
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at admin@apexsmmboosting.com.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data: bcrypt password hashing, JWT-signed sessions, TLS/HTTPS encryption, Cloudflare Turnstile bot protection, and IP-based brute force detection. No system is 100% secure — please use a strong, unique password for your account.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of ApexBoost after changes constitutes your acceptance.

9. Contact

For privacy-related concerns, contact us at admin@apexsmmboosting.com.
