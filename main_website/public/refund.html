Refund Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026  ·  ApexSMM Boosting

Please read this policy carefully before making any deposit or placing orders. By using ApexBoost, you acknowledge and agree to the terms below.

1. General Policy

All purchases made on ApexBoost are generally non-refundable due to the nature of digital social media marketing services. Once an order is placed and processing has begun, the service cannot be reversed.

However, we are committed to fairness. Refunds or wallet credits may be issued in specific circumstances outlined below.

2. Refund Eligibility

✓ Eligible for Refund/Credit
  • Order was never started by the provider
  • Service was delivered significantly less than ordered (partial refund)
  • Duplicate order placed due to a system error
  • Deposit credited incorrectly due to our error
  • Service was marked complete but not delivered
✗ Not Eligible for Refund
  • Order is in progress or already completed
  • Followers/likes dropped after delivery (natural fluctuation)
  • Wrong URL submitted by the user
  • Account was banned by the social media platform
  • Change of mind after order placement
  • Violation of our Terms of Service

3. Wallet Credits vs. Cash Refunds

Eligible refunds are issued as wallet credits on your ApexBoost account by default. Cash refunds (back to GCash/Maya/BPI) are only issued in exceptional cases where:

  • A deposit was made but could not be credited due to our system error
  • You request account deletion with an unused wallet balance
Cash refunds may take 3–7 business days to process. Wallet credits are typically applied within 24 hours.

4. Drop Protection

Some services include drop protection or refill guarantees within a specified period (shown on the service listing). If your order drops below the delivered quantity within the refill window, you may request a free refill — not a cash refund.

5. How to Request a Refund

To submit a refund request:

  • Open a support ticket from your dashboard
  • Include your Order ID, the issue description, and any supporting screenshots
  • Our team will review your request within 1–3 business days
Refund requests submitted more than 14 days after the order completion date will not be accepted.

6. Chargebacks

Initiating a chargeback with your bank or GCash/Maya without first contacting ApexBoost support is considered a violation of our Terms of Service. Your account will be permanently suspended and the wallet balance forfeited if a fraudulent chargeback is filed.

7. Contact Us

For refund-related inquiries, reach us at admin@apexsmmboosting.com or via the support ticket system in your dashboard.
